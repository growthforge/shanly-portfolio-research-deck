FIG 1.1 · Sheet 01 of 05 · MTest Labs⌐ docked · esc ✕
Case

Test design went from days to a supervised morning.

Who needed it
A testing team writing test designs by hand for every release.
What changed
A simple drafting tool produces the first pass. A person reviews, edits and signs off every design.
What happened
Design time moved from 8 hours, or 2 to 3 days on larger pieces, to 1 to 3 hours supervised.
