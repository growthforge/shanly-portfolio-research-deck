## Cross-platform takeaways (for the deck)
1. **Hero**: eyebrow (mono, small caps) → oversized headline with a concrete claim → 2 CTAs (filled + outline) → hairline stat strip (21st hero-15 + Tailark).
2. **Proof chips**: bento stat tiles with big numeral + tiny uppercase label (21st stats-bento); status pill with dot (feature-bento); avatar stack + stars (Aceternity).
3. **Case sheets / index**: registry.directory row list (title, tiny tag line, right-aligned meta, hairline dividers) is the closest "warm paper" language found.
4. **Process timeline**: Aceternity Timeline, Shadcnblocks timeline, Magic UI animated beam as connector; keep motion minimal.
5. **Mood/texture**: Recent.design (ex-Godly) and Land-book for cream/serif/illustrated references.
