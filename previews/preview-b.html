01 · MTest Labs: test design tool

AI Solutions Consultant · from Dec 2025

Software testing firm · client build

What I built
An internal tool with its own interface that generates tailored test scenarios and cases, plus a conversational assistant so the team can query the firm's knowledge.
Why
Test design was manual and slow: a full 8 hour day, or 2 to 3 days on larger projects.
How
It gathers full project context from the firm's documentation and a structured question flow, then drafts through a staged, multi-agent orchestration grounded in the firm's test-case library and historical issue logs. Refined in build, test and improve cycles with the testers.
Who uses it
The testers. I trained the team to run it themselves and left documentation. Person reviews: a tester approves every output before anything reaches a client.
Result
Test design cut to 1 to 3 hours of supervised draft and review.

Beforeread documents → write cases by hand → a full day, or 2 to 3 days

Afterdocuments + questions → staged agents draft → person reviews → client

02 · Atelier di Venice: connected workflows

from Dec 2025

Interior design business · client build

What I built
Dozens of automated workflows across operations, marketing (including lead follow-up) and finance. A Power BI dashboard replaced a rekeyed monthly management pack.
Why
Operations, marketing and finance steps were handled separately and manually, from first enquiry through to delivery.
How
Mapped the business end to end, then connected the separate steps with low-code automation and APIs. Set an autonomy boundary: low-risk steps run alone, consequential client and financial steps stay supervised.
Who uses it
The owner. I trained the owner to run the system day to day.
Result
Dozens of workflows automated; the monthly pack is now a dashboard.

Beforeenquiry / ops / marketing / finance, each by hand → pack rekeyed monthly

Aftermapped → connected → low-risk runs alone → person reviews client and financial steps → Power BI

Build, buy or simplify: a check for existing software

  1. What job does it do, and who does that job today?
  2. Can the step be removed or simplified first?
  3. Can Microsoft 365, Power Platform or Copilot already do it?
  4. Can the IT Manager and external IT partners support it?
  5. Can we trial it small with the people who will use it?
  6. Who owns it, and where is it documented?
